## Book Description (Long Blurb) — Draft Template
**She built a life on philanthropy.**

Dr. Mara Vale knows how power hides: behind gala lights, donor walls, and smiling board members who never put anything in writing. When a survivor-coded warning appears in a gift bag at her own charity event, Mara realizes the truth she’s been circling for years:

**The monster isn’t a person. It’s an ecosystem.**

To map it, she creates a second identity—**Hale**, a concierge fixer who can enter rooms Mara never could. But every door Hale opens demands a price: favors traded in silence, evidence that evaporates, and a reputation machine that turns victims into liars.

As the invitations escalate from private lounges to off-book airfields and “wellness retreats,” Mara’s two selves begin to bleed together. And the network that thrives on secrets starts to notice something worse than an enemy.

It notices a recruit.
