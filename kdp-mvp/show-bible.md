# Show Bible — *Working Title: THE HYDE PROTOCOL*

## 0) Logline
A brilliant, philanthropic biotech fixer builds a second identity to infiltrate an elite trafficking-and-blackmail pipeline—only to discover the machine isn’t run by a single monster, but by an **ecosystem** of enablers who profit from secrecy.

## 1) Series Promise
A modern *Jekyll/Hyde* thriller where:
- **Jekyll** = public virtue, philanthropy, access, clean hands.
- **Hyde** = shadow operations, information warfare, and morally corrosive tactics.
- The antagonist is not one villain; it’s an **Epstein-energy ecosystem**: an interlocking network of prestige, logistics, kompromat, and reputational laundering.

The emotional hook: the protagonist’s internal split deepens until they must decide **what kind of monster you’re allowed to become to stop monsters**.

## 2) Genre, Tone, and comps
- **Genre:** Conspiracy thriller / psychological noir / tech-tinged crime.
- **Tone:** Elegant dread, neon-cold luxury, courtroom-adjacent stakes without being a legal thriller.
- **Pacing:** Fast, episodic cliffhangers; each episode resolves a *surface* mystery while escalating the *core* conspiracy.
- **Vibes/comps (non-IP, descriptive):** “premium-cable investigative thriller meets dual-identity psychological descent.”

## 3) Core Theme Stack
1. **Ecosystems of harm:** evil scales via contracts, calendars, pilots, attorneys, assistants, security teams, and silence.
2. **Reputation as currency:** charity galas as laundering; awards as shields.
3. **Consent vs coercion:** power asymmetry, grooming-by-opportunity.
4. **Identity fracture:** the costs of undercover living; addiction to control.
5. **Truth vs proof:** knowing is not enough; you need *evidence that survives the machine*.

## 4) Setting
A fictional coastal metropolis, **Port Meridian**, where:
- Oceanfront mansions sit near a high-security private airfield.
- A “philanthropic corridor” of museums and research institutes shares donors, boards, and PR firms.
- Private security, concierge medicine, and boutique law firms form a **shadow public sector**.

## 5) The Ecosystem (antagonist model)
Use this as a repeating “machine” the protagonist keeps mapping.

### 5.1 Roles in the pipeline
- **The Patron(s):** money + appetite; insulated by charity and boards.
- **The Curator:** identifies targets, builds dependence, orchestrates introductions.
- **The Logistics Wing:** drivers, pilots, apartment managers, “event coordinators.”
- **The Gatekeepers:** doormen, assistants, personal chiefs-of-staff who control access.
- **The Cleaners:** NDAs, settlements, threats, reputation management, blackmail distribution.
- **The Audience:** adjacent elites who “don’t ask,” trading silence for proximity.

### 5.2 Signature tactics (“Epstein-energy” without direct reference)
- **Invitation ladders:** favors → access → obligations.
- **Prestige traps:** scholarships, fellowships, internships that become cages.
- **Off-book travel:** private airfield, yacht charters, “medical retreats.”
- **Kompromat manufacturing:** curated parties, controlled substances, staged photographs.
- **Reputational laundering:** charity events, museum wings, think-tank panels.

## 6) Main Characters

### Dr. Mara Vale (Jekyll)
- **Public face:** celebrated biotech philanthropist; funds addiction recovery tech.
- **Private wound:** grew up adjacent to a “benefactor” who bought her silence.
- **Superpower:** pattern recognition, social engineering, and cold calm.
- **Fatal flaw:** believes she can outsmart systems without becoming them.

### “Hale” (Hyde persona)
- **Constructed identity:** a ruthless concierge-fixer specializing in “discreet solutions.”
- **Function:** gets Mara into rooms she can’t enter as herself.
- **Cost:** the persona starts to feel more “real” than Mara.

### Jonas Creed
- **Role:** investigative journalist turned independent researcher.
- **Trait:** allergic to hero narratives; obsessed with chain-of-custody.
- **Dynamic:** ally with mistrust; he suspects Mara is part of the machine.

### Selene Arkady
- **Role:** luxury PR strategist; ecosystem’s reputational engineer.
- **Weapon:** narrative; she doesn’t destroy you, she makes you unbelievable.

### Bishop Rook
- **Role:** private security contractor; former military logistics.
- **Weapon:** access control, intimidation, plausible deniability.

### “The Benefactors” (antagonist ensemble)
A rotating set of donors, board members, and “visionaries.” No single face—only **patterns**.

## 7) Season 1 Arc (8-episode shape)
**Season question:** Can Mara expose a system designed to make exposure impossible?

- **E1 — The Invitation:** Mara receives a survivor-coded warning inside a gala gift.
- **E2 — The Airfield:** Hale gains access to private-flight manifests; evidence vanishes.
- **E3 — The Retreat:** A wellness institute is a capture site; a rescue becomes a trap.
- **E4 — The Settlement:** A hush agreement reveals the legal “plumbing.”
- **E5 — The Museum Wing:** Reputational laundering; donors weaponize art institutions.
- **E6 — The Handler:** The Curator is identified—then replaced.
- **E7 — The Dead Drop:** A public drop forces the ecosystem to counterattack.
- **E8 — The Split:** Mara must choose: burn herself publicly or keep Hale alive.

## 8) Episode engine (repeatable structure)
Each episode should contain:
1. **Cold open:** a luxury-adjacent scene that reveals coercion without explicitness.
2. **A clue tied to logistics** (travel, keys, calendars, NDAs, invoices).
3. **A dual-identity dilemma** where Mara (Jekyll) can’t act, but Hale (Hyde) can.
4. **A moral compromise** that gets results and deepens fracture.
5. **A cliffhanger**: proof obtained, but chain-of-custody threatened.

## 9) Content boundaries (recommended)
- Keep depiction of exploitation **non-graphic**; focus on coercion mechanics and aftermath.
- Center survivors’ agency and ambiguity (what they choose vs what was engineered).
- Never imply real-world individuals. Use composite archetypes only.

## 10) Visual motifs (for prose + cover language)
- **Mirrors / reflections** (identity split)
- **White gloves** (clean hands, dirty work)
- **Airfield lights** (off-book travel)
- **Gala orchids** (beauty as camouflage)
- **Burner phones** (truth that self-destructs)
