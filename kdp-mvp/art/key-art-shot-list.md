# Key Art Shot List (Cover + Promo)

## Design goals
- Communicate: **dual identity**, **elite luxury**, **cold conspiracy logistics**.
- Avoid literal/graphic imagery; keep it **noir, elegant, ominous**.
- Color story: black/white + one accent (orchid purple, airfield amber, or surgical teal).

---

## Primary Cover Concepts (choose 1 direction)

### Concept A — “Mirror Split” (Jekyll/Hyde)
1. Close-up of a face in profile, split by a mirror edge; reflection shows different expression.
2. Gloved hand holding a burner phone; the screen reflects a gala chandelier.
3. Orchid petals drifting across a black field; petals form a subtle split circle.

### Concept B — “Luxury Logistics” (ecosystem)
4. Minimalist airfield at night: runway lights receding into darkness; a silhouette of a private jet tail.
5. A leather portfolio stamped with an abstract seal; a corner of a boarding pass peeks out.
6. A concierge desk bell beside a nondisclosure contract—both lit like evidence.

### Concept C — “Philanthropy as Camouflage”
7. Gala centerpiece (orchids) with a hidden keycard tucked under the ribbon.
8. Museum wall plaque (“Donated by…”) with names scratched out; security camera reflection.
9. A donation receipt that, when folded, becomes the split circle symbol.

---

## Series Branding Elements (consistent across episodes)
- A small **split circle** glyph on every cover spine or lower-right corner.
- Episode color accents:
  - Ep1: Orchid purple
  - Ep2: Runway amber
  - Ep3: Clinical teal
- Typography: sharp modern serif for title + clean sans for subtitle.

---

## Episode-Specific Shot List

### Episode 1 — “The Invitation”
1. Gift bag silhouette with orchid stem; burner phone glow.
2. A gala chandelier reflected in a wine glass; distortion suggests a second face.
3. A keycard half-hidden under a place setting card.

### Episode 2 — “The Airfield”
4. Runway lights in fog; a black SUV headlights flare.
5. A maintenance logbook with a tail number redacted; ink smear looks like wings.
6. Hangar door gap with a sliver of jet fuselage and a security camera LED.

### Episode 3 — “The Retreat”
7. Ocean-cliff modernist spa architecture; too-perfect calm.
8. Phone lockbox with numbered slots; one slot empty.
9. Consent packet pages clipped; the paper casts a prison-bar shadow.
