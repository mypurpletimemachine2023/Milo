# Episode 3 Outline — “The Retreat”

## One-line
A luxury wellness institute reveals itself as a capture-and-compliance site, and Mara’s attempt to rescue Lina forces her to choose between **public integrity** and **private effectiveness**.

## Structure
5-part escalation: cold open, acts I–IV, tag.

## Cold Open
Inside the **Aster House Institute** (a cliffside wellness campus):
- Guests surrender phones “for nervous system regulation.”
- A therapist’s questions sound like care but track vulnerabilities.
- A guest signs a consent packet with an arbitration clause and a “voluntary residential” addendum.

## Act I — The Schedule
1. **The clock:** Jonas shows Mara the photo email—Lina’s timestamp is tomorrow.
2. **Target:** Aster House sits between health spa and private detention.
3. **Mara’s PR crisis:** A headline breaks: *FOUNDATION GRANTS USED FOR PAYOFFS?*
4. **Mara’s choice:** She will attend Aster House publicly as a donor—while Hale goes in as a fixer.

## Act II — Two Doors, Same Trap
5. **Mara’s entry (Jekyll):** a donor tour with Selene as host.
   - Selene frames Aster House as “trauma-informed excellence.”
   - Mara notices physical control: keycards, controlled sightlines, staff radios.
6. **Hale’s entry (Hyde):** hired to “solve” a disruptive guest.
   - Hale is given a staff lanyard that opens the wrong doors (a test).
7. **Lina spotted:** Lina is on campus, coached to smile.
   - She is being prepped for “travel.”

## Act III — The Rescue Attempt
8. **Contact:** Mara slips Lina a note: *Bathroom vent, 9:10.*
9. **Complication:** Lina is monitored by a “wellness companion” (handler).
10. **Hale’s leverage:** Hale blackmails a mid-level administrator with procurement fraud.
   - Gains access to intake files and “voluntary residential” signatures.
11. **Jonas’s constraint:** He needs a document with undeniable authenticity.
12. **The document:** Hale finds a template packet—pre-signed blanks, notarized in advance.
   - This is the mechanism: consent manufactured.

## Act IV — The Ecosystem’s Countermove
13. **Trap sprung:** Security locks down a corridor; an announcement: “Guest safety drill.”
14. **Mara confronted:** Selene privately warns: “Your brand can’t survive a scandal. Do you want to save one girl or keep saving thousands?”
15. **Hale confronted:** Rook appears, calm. “You’re not undercover. You’re auditioning.”
16. **Mara’s dark decision:** To extract Lina, Mara agrees (publicly) to announce a partnership grant with Aster House—buying temporary access.
   - Jonas is horrified: she’s laundering them.
17. **Extraction:** Hale uses the chaos of the press moment to route Lina through staff passages.
18. **Cost:** Lina escapes—but the partnership announcement goes viral.

## Tag / Cliffhanger
19. **Aftermath:** Jonas refuses to publish without certainty; the ecosystem’s narrative is winning.
20. **Final stinger:** Mara watches Hale in the mirror, and for a second the reflection moves wrong.
   - On Mara’s phone: a message from an unknown number:
     > WE LIKE HALE. WE CAN USE HALE.
**End beat:** The ecosystem is no longer reacting; it’s recruiting.

## Episode deliverables
- Authentic-looking consent/arbitration packet templates (institutional control).
- Proof-of-mechanism: manufactured consent + controlled communications.
- Public reputational compromise (partnership announcement) raising stakes.

## Episode theme
Rescue can become laundering. Winning one life can cost the war.
