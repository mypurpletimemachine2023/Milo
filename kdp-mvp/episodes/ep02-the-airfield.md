# Episode 2 Outline — “The Airfield”

## One-line
Hale infiltrates a private aviation hub to obtain flight manifests—only to learn the ecosystem’s favorite trick is **erasing the paper** and corrupting the witnesses.

## Structure
4 acts + tag; escalating operational tension.

## Cold Open
A night mechanic at **Meridian Executive Air** signs off on a maintenance log.
- The tail number doesn’t match the hangar.
- A supervisor says: “It’s a courtesy.”
- A camera blinks off for exactly 90 seconds.

## Act I — Badge Games
1. **Mara vs Hale:** Mara rehearses being “normal,” while Hale rehearses being feared.
2. **Bishop Rook introduced (properly):** Hale meets him under the guise of contracting “discretion.”
   - Rook offers a warning: “At this altitude, truth needs a sponsor.”
3. **Objective defined:** Jonas wants manifests + chain-of-custody; Mara wants Lina safe.
4. **Plan:** Hale will penetrate the airfield’s vendor ecosystem (catering, maintenance, fuel).

## Act II — The Paper That Isn’t Paper
5. **Infiltration:** Hale bribes into a vendor tour, plants a benign “audit” narrative.
6. **Discovery:** The airfield uses a dual system:
   - Official logs (clean)
   - “Courtesy slots” (off-book) coordinated via encrypted concierge service.
7. **Selene’s pressure:** Selene invites Mara (as herself) to a museum planning lunch—public proximity as leverage.
8. **Lina’s spiral:** Lina receives a “friendly” text: *We’re glad you’re traveling again.*
   - She never agreed to travel.

## Act III — Evidence and Contamination
9. **Score:** Hale copies a week of courtesy-slot metadata (not full names—patterns).
10. **Complication:** A security sweep starts early. Rook suspects an internal leak.
11. **Jonas’s rule:** Evidence must be verifiable without Hale’s word. They need a second source.
12. **Second source:** The night mechanic from cold open agrees to talk—until he demands payment.
13. **Mara’s compromise:** Mara pays via her foundation under a “hardship grant,” muddying ethics.
14. **Twist:** The mechanic delivers a USB… but it’s a decoy designed to “prove” Mara forged it.

## Act IV — The Airfield Bites Back
15. **Public smear seed:** Selene quietly tips a blogger: “Mara Vale is funding hush money.”
16. **Private threat:** Lina’s safe apartment is entered—nothing stolen, only moved.
   - Message: *We know where she sleeps; we know where you think.*
17. **Hale’s retaliation:** Hale confronts Rook with a bluff—claims to have already duplicated the manifest.
   - Rook doesn’t flinch: “Then you know it’s not the planes. It’s the invitations.”
18. **Reframe:** The real asset isn’t the flight list; it’s the **concierge network** that routes bodies, favors, and secrets.

## Tag / Cliffhanger
19. **Jonas receives an anonymous email:** “If you want names, come alone.”
   - Attached: a photo of Lina at the townhouse from Episode 1—timestamped *tomorrow*.
**End beat:** Lina is scheduled, like cargo.

## Episode deliverables
- Courtesy-slot metadata showing repeating patterns (times, routes, vendors).
- Introduction of concierge network as core mechanism.
- Smear campaign planted against Mara.

## Episode theme
Proof isn’t just facts; it’s **trust infrastructure**. The ecosystem attacks both.
