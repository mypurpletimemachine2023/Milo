# Episode 1 Outline — “The Invitation”

## One-line
A charity gala delivers Mara Vale a survivor-coded warning—and a map to the ecosystem’s first pressure valve: **access**.

## POV + structure
- Third-person close on Mara; occasional intercut with “Hale” scenes.
- 4 acts + tag.

## Cold Open (2–4 pages)
**Scene:** A chauffeured car drops a young scholarship finalist, **Lina**, at a marble-front townhouse.
- The doorman knows her name; the assistant corrects her outfit (“photographs better”).
- Lina signs a “standard release” that’s really a broad NDA.
- She pockets a tiny card with a hand-drawn symbol (a split circle).
**Cut:** The door shuts like a vault.

## Act I — The Gala Gift
1. **Gala setup:** Mara hosts the **Vale Foundation** benefit for addiction recovery tech.
   - She’s praised for “saving lives,” but her body remembers being bought.
2. **Inciting incident:** A donor’s assistant hands Mara a gift bag “from the board.”
   - Inside: an orchid, a burner phone, and a note: **YOU CAN’T OUT-DONATE THEM. OUT-LOGISTICS THEM.**
3. **First clue:** The note includes a sketch of the split circle symbol + a date/time.
4. **Refusal:** Mara’s security chief says it’s a prank; PR wants to bury it.
5. **Choice:** Mara turns on the burner.

## Act II — Building Hale
6. **The call:** A distorted voice gives Mara a single instruction: “Go as someone else.”
7. **Mara’s fracture:** She remembers her own “benefactor” era—how kindness was a leash.
8. **Hyde construction montage:**
   - Mara creates **Hale**: forged references, wardrobe, a new voice, a new posture.
   - She repurposes her research lab’s access-control expertise into social access.
9. **Jonas Creed introduced:** He corners Mara at the gala, skeptical of her purity.
   - He’s tracking disappearances around “wellness fellowships.”
   - He shows Mara the split circle symbol in a leaked screenshot.
10. **Deal:** Mara offers Jonas data access; Jonas offers chain-of-custody discipline.

## Act III — The First Door
11. **The meet:** Hale arrives at a private lounge above a members-only restaurant.
   - **Selene Arkady** is holding court, shaping narratives.
12. **Test:** Selene asks Hale to fix a “problem” quietly (a video leak of a donor’s son).
   - Hale refuses the job—but offers a *better* fix: identify the leaker.
   - Selene smiles: she likes predators who pretend they aren’t.
13. **Breadcrumb:** Selene mentions an upcoming “retreat” and a “manifest issue.”
14. **Meanwhile, Mara:** at home, she watches Hale footage and feels dissociation—Hale is competent in ways Mara isn’t allowed to be.

## Act IV — The Warning Has a Name
15. **Lina returns:** Lina shows up at Mara’s foundation office, shaken but not broken.
   - She recognizes Mara from a “board dinner.” She thought Mara was part of it.
16. **Revelation:** Lina confirms the split circle is used as a *signal* among targets: “If you see it, you’re being sorted.”
17. **The ask:** Lina wants out—but the ecosystem has her scholarship, housing, and visa help locked.
18. **Mara’s compromise:** Mara offers Lina a safe apartment under her foundation’s name.
   - Jonas warns: that makes Lina a “sponsor-owned asset.”
19. **Countermove:** Mara uses Hale to request “manifest help” from Bishop Rook’s security team.

## Tag / Cliffhanger
20. **Delivery:** A courier drops a sealed envelope at Mara’s door.
   - Inside: a private airfield access badge in Lina’s name… and a photo of Mara from years ago with her old benefactor’s hand on her shoulder.
**End beat:** The ecosystem knows who she is—and it’s inviting her back in.

## Episode deliverables (evidence gained)
- Symbol (split circle) associated with selection/grooming pipeline.
- First mention of **private airfield manifests**.
- Lina as a living witness + vulnerability vector.

## Episode theme
You can’t fight a machine with charity. You need **access**—and access changes you.
