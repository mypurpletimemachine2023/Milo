# Business Plan (One Page) — BigEyes Gym Fuel

## Mission
Make high-protein nutrition feel simple and fun through viral, character-driven short videos.

## Audience
- Beginners lifting 3–5x/week
- Busy parents & workers who want muscle + health
- People who need easy protein ideas

## Value proposition
- “Protein targets made simple” + snack/meal system
- Cute emotional big-eyed characters = high shareability

## Content engine
- 1 niche page = 1 clear algorithm signal
- Daily output: 1–3 reels
- Weekly: compile best topics into ebook chapters

## Distribution
- Start: Facebook Reels + Instagram Reels
- Repurpose: YouTube Shorts + TikTok

## Monetization
- Lead magnet: free 7-day plan (email capture)
- Low ticket: KDP ebook ($7–$19) + PDF direct sale
- Mid ticket: 30-day plan bundle
- Later: lightweight app / subscription

## KPIs
- View-to-click: 0.5–2% (early)
- Email opt-in: 20–40% (with strong lead magnet)
- Ebook conversion: 1–5% (from email + retarget)

## Risk management
- Avoid trademarked terms (Pixar/Disney)
- Avoid medical claims
- Use consistent original characters/branding

1) Launch 1 channel + landing page
2) Post 14 reels (2/day)
3) Build lead magnet PDF
4) Build v1 KDP ebook outline + 20 illustrations
