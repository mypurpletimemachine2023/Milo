# KDP Ebook Blueprint — BigEyes Gym Fuel (v1)

## Working title options
1) BigEyes Gym Fuel: Feed Your Muscles with Protein
2) BigEyes Gym Fuel: High‑Protein Meals & Snacks for Busy Lifters
3) BigEyes Gym Fuel: The Cute Meal Plan That Gets You Strong

## Format
- KDP Kindle + Paperback
- 80–120 pages (start smaller if needed)
- Full color illustrations (generated) + simple charts

## Sections (chapters)
1) **Gym Fuel Basics** (what protein does, why it matters)
2) **Protein Targets** (rules of thumb, per-meal targets)
3) **Leucine & MPS** (simple explanation + food examples)
4) **Timing** (pre vs post workout, daily distribution)
5) **Snack System** (grab-and-go high protein)
6) **Budget Gym Fuel** (cheap protein list)
7) **30 Recipes / Snack Builds** (simple ingredients)
8) **7-Day Meal Plan** + shopping lists
9) **Trackers** (printable pages)
10) **FAQs + disclaimers**

## Image plan (Nano-banana)
- 1 cover character (mascot)
- 1 chapter opener per chapter (9 total)
- 30 recipe/snack character illustrations (one per item)
- 5 simple infographic-style images (macro plate, per-meal target, etc.)

## Disclaimer (KDP-safe)
This book is for educational purposes only and is not medical advice.
