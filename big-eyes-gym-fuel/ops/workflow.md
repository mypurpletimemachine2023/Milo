# Workflow: BigEyes Gym Fuel

## Daily production (30–60 min)

### Step 1 — Pick the batch theme (choose 1)
- Protein basics (grams/day, per-meal targets)
- Leucine trigger & MPS
- Pre-workout vs post-workout fueling
- High-protein snacks
- Budget protein
- Muscle-building breakfast

### Step 2 — Create a 10-pack
For each concept:
- **Hook line** (on-screen text to add later in CapCut)
- **Nano-banana prompt** (hero frame)
- **Sora prompt** (8–12s video)
- **CTA** (comment/pinned)

### Step 3 — Generate assets
- Generate 1 hero frame per concept (Nano-banana)
- Generate 1 video per concept (Sora) using the hero frame as reference if possible

### Step 4 — Edit (CapCut template)
- 9:16, 8–12s
- Captions: 1 hook + 2 facts + CTA
- Sound: trending audio low (-18 to -24 LUFS), SFX subtle
- End card (0.5s): “Free 7-day plan — link in bio”

### Step 5 — Post & log
Log into a simple sheet or the MILO dashboard later:
- Date
- Concept slug
- Platform
- Views/likes/comments/saves
- Winner/loser tag

## Weekly compilation
- Pick top 10 performing concepts
- Expand into 1 chapter of the ebook (more facts + recipes/snack list)
- Generate 10 matching illustrations (Nano-banana)

## Compliance notes
- Don’t claim to cure/treat disease.
- Prefer phrasing like: “supports muscle protein synthesis,” “may help,” “generally recommended.”
- Add ebook disclaimer: educational, not medical advice.
