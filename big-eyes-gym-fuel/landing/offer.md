# Offer Ladder: BigEyes Gym Fuel

## Primary funnel
Reel/Short -> Landing page -> Email capture (freebie) -> Ebook purchase -> Upsell bundle

## Products
### Free
- **Free PDF**: “7-Day Gym Fuel Meal Plan + Shopping List” (lead magnet)

### Low ticket ($7–$19)
- **KDP ebook**: “BigEyes Gym Fuel: 30 High-Protein Meals & Snacks + Macro Cheat Sheets”
- Also sell as PDF on landing page (higher margin)

### Mid ticket ($19–$49)
- **30-day plan**: meal plan + shopping lists + macros + habit tracker

### Later
- Lightweight web app: meal plan generator + shopping list + macro targets

## Landing page (simple)
Sections:
1) Hero: “Feed Your Muscles (without boring food)”
2) Proof: “Protein targets made simple” (bullets)
3) What you get: plan, recipes/snacks, cheat sheets, grocery lists
4) CTA: Free plan download + ebook offer
5) FAQ + disclaimer

## CTA copy (short)
- “Free 7-day plan in bio.”
- “Grab the full Gym Fuel ebook (recipes + macros) — link in bio.”
