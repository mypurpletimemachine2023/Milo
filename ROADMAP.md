# Roadmap (30/60/90)

## 0–30 days (Ship MVPs)
- Lead capture + routing: website form + SMS + inbox + call tracking numbers
- Jobs pipeline: simple dispatch board + job statuses + notes/photos
- Reviews flywheel: automated review request + unhappy-path handling
- Local SEO: money pages + internal linking + GBP alignment

## 31–60 days (Optimize + Automate)
- Quote guardrails + service catalog + pricing ranges
- Technician analytics: close rate, time-to-dispatch, revenue attribution
- Content engine: weekly educational posts (lawful) + FAQs

## 61–90 days (Scale)
- Multi-tech scheduling + territory/coverage optimization
- Lightweight CRM: repeats, warranties, reminders (rekey follow-ups, smart lock maintenance)
- FederalLocksmith.com concept validation (directory + trust badge)
